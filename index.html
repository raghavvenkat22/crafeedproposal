A commercial proposal for performance marketing.

Scope, deliverables, and the structure of an engagement built on real ownership, considered decisions, and returns that compound.

I Scope of Work

Complete ownership of your performance marketing efforts.

We don't sit between dashboards and decks — we run the engine. Strategy, execution, optimisation, and accountability all live with the studio.

i

Strategy & Planning

An in-depth analysis of the business, target audience, and competitive landscape, leading to a comprehensive performance marketing strategy. This includes defining key performance indicators, setting up conversion tracking, and developing a detailed plan for channel allocation across Google Ads, Meta (Facebook / Instagram), and other relevant platforms.

ii

Campaign Management & Media Buying

The studio owns the day-to-day management of all paid advertising campaigns — audience targeting, budget allocation, bidding strategy, and continuous optimisation, with the singular intent of maximising return on ad spend and hitting defined marketing goals.

iii

Creative Strategy & Coordination

Data-driven insights and strategic direction for ad creatives. We analyse campaign performance to identify high-performing creative themes and write compelling hooks and ad copy, then coordinate directly with your internal or external designers to ensure the final assets are optimised for performance and aligned with brand messaging.

iv

Reporting & Analysis

Regular, transparent reporting on campaign performance — including detailed analysis of clicks, impressions, cost per acquisition, and ROAS at the campaign and ad levels. Weekly review calls are scheduled to walk through results, surface insights, and align on next steps.

v

Ongoing Optimisation & Strategy Refinement

Performance marketing is an iterative discipline. The studio continuously monitors campaigns and makes real-time adjustments — testing new audiences, refining ad creatives, and exploring new platforms — to ensure long-term, compounding growth.
