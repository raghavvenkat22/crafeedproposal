I
Strategy & Lead Generation

A lead engine, built to convert.

This is a lead-based setup. We draw qualified, intent-rich audiences from Meta and Google to a dedicated landing page, and we treat that page as the place the decision is actually made, not as an afterthought. Everything before it exists to deliver the right person; everything on it exists to convert them.

i
Audience & intent mapping
Defining who we want and why they would act, built around Saum's elevated, considered audience rather than broad, cheap reach.
ii
Campaign build across Meta & Google
A full lead-generation structure across Instagram, Facebook, and Google. Every campaign pointed at the landing page with a clear job to do.
iii
Landing page conversion strategy
The message, structure, and offer of the page where conversion happens, shaped and refined so traffic turns into enquiries, not bounces.
iv
Lead capture & qualification
Capture flows built so the leads that reach you are warm and worth the conversation; quality over raw volume.
v
Goal-setting & budget framework
Clear targets on cost per qualified lead, with a budget plan and forecast agreed before we begin.
II
Creative Strategy & Execution

Creative is the pivotal lever.

In a lead-based account, the creative is what decides whether a stranger stops, listens, and clicks. We don't just brief it. We strategise it and execute it, end to end. Getting the creative direction right is the single most important thing we will do for Saum, and we hold it to the brand's elevated, reverent register throughout.

i
Creative direction
A clear, performance-aware point of view that protects Saum's grace while still earning attention in a crowded feed, the foundation everything else is built on.
ii
Concepting & execution
We take creative from idea to finished, launch-ready asset: concepts, copy, and production coordinated and delivered, not merely outlined.
iii
Hook, format & placement testing
Structured testing of angles, formats, and placements to find what genuinely moves the audience, and to keep finding it as fatigue sets in.
iv
Landing page creative
Creative carried consistently from the ad through to the landing page, so the promise that earns the click is honoured by the page that converts it.
III
Reporting & Optimisation

Measured, then sharpened.

A lead engine is never finished; it is tuned. You receive an honest, plain-language view of what spend returns, and we use it to refine relentlessly, moving budget toward what works and retiring what doesn't.

i
Tracking & event setup
Conversion and lead-event tracking configured and quality-checked, so every number we report can be trusted.
ii
Weekly & monthly reporting
A concise weekly read on momentum, and a fuller monthly deep-dive with insight and clear next steps, with full transparency on spend and cost per lead.
iii
Continuous optimisation
Always-on testing across audience, creative, and landing page, with budget steered toward the best-performing combinations.
iv
Scaling & strategy reviews
Disciplined scaling of what works, and a regular step back to reassess direction against the season ahead.
